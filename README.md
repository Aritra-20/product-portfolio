## 🔍 Product teardowns

### [Microsoft Teams — from Slack challenger to default workplace](microsoft-teams-teardown.md)

[![Microsoft Teams teardown preview](teams-preview.jpg)](microsoft-teams-teardown.md)

A 23-slide teardown covering the user journey, three personas, the competitive landscape, a North Star metric, feature prioritisation and four product fixes.

- **The tension:** the centralisation that makes Teams indispensable is also what buries users in notifications.
- **North Star:** Weekly Active Collaborative Users (WACU), the % of *licensed* seats doing real collaboration each week.
- **Headline fix:** *Focus Digest*, an AI hub that batches low-priority pings and lets urgent @mentions through. I wireframed it in the deck.

## 📊 Commercial analytics case study

### [Pharma sales & rebate analytics — ProcDNA case](procdna-rebate-case-study.md)

Territory sales, system-level rebates and profit for a pharma brand, built in Excel from about 10,900 invoice lines. I fixed six data-quality issues before the numbers could be trusted.

- **Sales:** national weighted sales up **47%** in two quarters, mostly from one GPO group
- **Rebates:** **$7.25M** in Q1'25 across six GPO contracts plus the ICOP group contract, with ION alone taking 60%
- **Insight:** each non-340B unit clears $1,585, but rebates eat about **half of the $14.6M profit**. Rebate terms, not cost, are what move margin.

## 🛠️ How I work

1. **Start from the user's job, not the feature list.** Every teardown maps what each persona is actually trying to get done.
2. **Pick one metric that would move the business.** Then back it with a small set of health and guardrail metrics.
3. **Prioritise out loud.** Reach, impact and effort, scored and shown.
4. **Prototype the fix.** Even a rough wireframe beats a paragraph of recommendations.
